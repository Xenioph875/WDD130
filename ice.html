In-class Exercises

Week 5

In the Prophet Card assignment, I worked with a wireframe to create a finished product. It was a simple assignment that taughth me how to break up pieces of a website and build based off of them. The most difficult thing was sizing it.

Prophet Card

The Apostle Spotlight assignment was very similar to the prophet card. We had a picture to base the design off. There wasn't as much guidance on the picture as the previous assignment, so I was more dependent on what I learned in class. The most difficult thing was getting the positioning right. I learned a lot about how to use grid and the positioning functions within it.

Apostle Spotlight

Week 6

In this assignment, I learned how to create a table and input the data. We compiled a list of our favorite BYU and BYUI devotionals and displayed them into a table. Unfortunately, we did not cover the form, so the submit button does not work yet.

Favorite Devotional

Week 7

This was one of my favorite assignments! It was the perfect mix of fun and challenging. We create different div tags and put them into grids to create country flags. For the most part, it was really simple. The most difficult part was the position of the star in the C.A.R. flag. I also learned you can put a gap within the grid to give a buffer between elements.

Grid Flags

This positioning assignment was also really fun! In order to complete it, I had to look at the picture and break it down into a wireframe. That helped me understand what divs need to be placed where and which ones were on top. The most difficult part for me was activity 3 with the staggering boxes. I do not remember why it was so challenging, but something with the positioning or the size was throwing it off.

Positioning

Week 8

The Gallery assignment was very straight forward. I learned alot about images and resolution leading into it. I had to go through and resize the images so it did not take up so much space.

Gallery

The Sign Up assignment was a little challenging. I had to organize everything within the container. The positioning was giving me grief again. I was able to work through by setting the background image and a grid where the inputs are. The rest was a matter of sizing to get everything to fit and look right.
